# G1 identity and G3 bandwidth (GPU evidence, first hardware run)

Date 2026-10-17. Both cards released by the user. Tool: `dualgpu-probe` (CUDA 12.4.1 build, SM86), driver DLL 32.0.16.1714.
Preflight: no llama-server, no listener on 8080-8082/8093, no compute process on the 3060.

## G1 identity

| CUDA index | nvidia-smi index | Card | PCI | UUID | CC | Total MiB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | RTX 3070 | 0000:08:00.0 | dacb7330-a55d-88d3-7a2c-708067e4bda6 | 8.6 | 8191 |
| 1 | 0 | RTX 3060 | 0000:04:00.0 | 05fe89f8-3b7f-bad5-bea0-7f29915dc02c | 8.6 | 12287 |

CUDA order is the reverse of nvidia-smi (as in the Swift ledger): 3070 = CUDA0, 3060 = CUDA1. Peer access: none in either direction, so all-reduce copies go through host memory.
nvidia-smi link at the time: 3070 Gen2 x16 idle (max Gen4), 3060 Gen1 x4 idle (max Gen3). Widths match the Windows PnP read.

## G3 pinned host bandwidth (256 MiB x 20, cudaHostAllocPortable)

| Card | H2D GB/s | D2H GB/s |
|---|---|---|
| RTX 3070 (CUDA0) | 25.2-25.7 | 25.0-25.7 |
| RTX 3060 (CUDA1) | 2.84 | 3.26 |

Both cards at once (separate threads): 25.3 / 25.4 and 2.84 / 3.26. No measurable interference. 64 MiB x 40 gives the same numbers.
The 3060 link is about 9x slower than the 3070 link. This supports the plan's head map (more KV heads on the 3070, the 3060 shard resident).

## Memory finding that affects the model plan

nvidia-smi at preflight: RTX 3070 4132 MiB used / 3886 MiB free (many desktop apps: browsers, chat apps, Docker Desktop, LM Studio, overlays).
RTX 3060 23 MiB used / 12091 MiB free. `cudaMemGetInfo` reported 7113 MiB free on the 3070 (WDDM budget view); nvidia-smi is the conservative figure and is what the 512 MiB floor uses.
The Swift ledger recorded 209 MiB idle on the 3070. With today's desktop load, UD-Q4_K_S (14.30 GiB) plus overhead does not fit under the floor: free total 15962 MiB, weights alone 14646 MiB.
Needed before the model run: lower the 3070 desktop GPU load by about 3 GiB or more.
